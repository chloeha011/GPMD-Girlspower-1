# Our Deliverables

**Team:** Girlspower-1 (GPMD) | **Module:** Algorithms, Data Structures, and Connectionist AI

Our team built one learning module for beginners. The Markdown content in this repository is the shared source, and it is turned into three learning resources: one PowerPoint, one Study Guide with Test Bank, and four video tutorials. Each resource presents the same ideas in a different way, so learners can pick the one that suits them.

## At a Glance

| # | Deliverable | What it is | Best used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **PPT Slides** (1 file) | Slide deck covering algorithms, data structures and connectionist AI | Classroom teaching and quick revision |
| 2 | **Study Guide and Test Bank** (1 file) | Eight chapters of explanations, examples and activities, followed by a 70-question test bank with explained answers | Independent study and self-testing |
| 3 | **Video Tutorials** (4 videos) | Short videos that explain and demonstrate the key ideas | Learning by watching and following along |
